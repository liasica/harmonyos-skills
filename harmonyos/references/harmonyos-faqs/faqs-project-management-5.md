使用代码模板可以提高开发效率。在DevEco Studio中，可以通过“File \> Settings \> Editor \> Live Templates”查看现有模板并新增自定义模板。

![](https://contentcenter-vali-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/pvt_2/DeveloperAlliance_scene_100_1/87/v3/any7v5npRmCMaerLb0RqvA/zh-cn_image_0000002654797791.png?HW-CC-KV=V1&HW-CC-Date=20261112T233217Z&HW-CC-Expire=31536000000&HW-CC-Sign=687D81D735D5C5F56BE78DB18268FBC0D4CBE6710E3ACBA077494A7CD0931211 "点击放大")

点击展开按钮查看现有模板详情，点击右上角加号新增自定义模板。

2. 自定义代码模板。

   ![](https://contentcenter-vali-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/pvt_2/DeveloperAlliance_scene_100_1/22/v3/JWyGJup9QOyY9jCZL8DUaQ/zh-cn_image_0000002654797795.png?HW-CC-KV=V1&HW-CC-Date=20261112T233217Z&HW-CC-Expire=31536000000&HW-CC-Sign=B0CBB6541519D8A2B4F76EDDF1407F0B13A0C3699D96B3905AA0D89B8D693028 "点击放大")![](https://contentcenter-vali-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/pvt_2/DeveloperAlliance_scene_100_1/6a/v3/46yON6U3T_O6uu45E_nOIg/zh-cn_image_0000002624638336.png?HW-CC-KV=V1&HW-CC-Date=20261112T233217Z&HW-CC-Expire=31536000000&HW-CC-Sign=1B453FB1C9EEAA4437DAE89A308E7DD3E2B3751772B7AE915BA2A56E5B0A2D85 "点击放大")

   ![](https://contentcenter-vali-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/pvt_2/DeveloperAlliance_scene_100_1/36/v3/Oi4EPbDoRR6XrQlxZ6SHqA/zh-cn_image_0000002654837741.png?HW-CC-KV=V1&HW-CC-Date=20261112T233217Z&HW-CC-Expire=31536000000&HW-CC-Sign=34BE63BCDC92D40EC531D787A422EF0CF3C8DB665F768193617DDF1D4438CCEF "点击放大")

   选中自定义群组，点击+，新增模板。

   ![](https://contentcenter-vali-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/pvt_2/DeveloperAlliance_scene_100_1/68/v3/w9Ayljw1TCekYDOEjGBSew/zh-cn_image_0000002624478430.png?HW-CC-KV=V1&HW-CC-Date=20261112T233217Z&HW-CC-Expire=31536000000&HW-CC-Sign=399AB823A34E50024077C1B55ED86CA2F055BF59366ED32C26C960E0F9B1C249 "点击放大")

   ![](https://contentcenter-vali-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/pvt_2/DeveloperAlliance_scene_100_1/8e/v3/lMdcQXSZTPOP4TnOJ1UmDg/zh-cn_image_0000002654797797.png?HW-CC-KV=V1&HW-CC-Date=20261112T233217Z&HW-CC-Expire=31536000000&HW-CC-Sign=182692EE144EECEA90E42546B7D16507FB54D955317CE34ACACBB427E33C042B "点击放大")

   点击上图的“Change”处，选择代码模板文件。

   ![](https://contentcenter-vali-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/pvt_2/DeveloperAlliance_scene_100_1/03/v3/sf9JSNHiTYeVsjmZL8xLxA/zh-cn_image_0000002624638338.png?HW-CC-KV=V1&HW-CC-Date=20261112T233217Z&HW-CC-Expire=31536000000&HW-CC-Sign=A98C0636171560331E8C9DC8CFD0163B9FD2A4902EED6E4C3B089318DE36508E "点击放大")
