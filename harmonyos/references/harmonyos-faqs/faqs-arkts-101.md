1. 在类内部，右键点击，选择“生成”。

   ![](https://contentcenter-vali-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/pvt_2/DeveloperAlliance_scene_100_1/6a/v3/_LQBzDT5RAqy8XDTRPy5Gg/zh-cn_image_0000002654835185.png "点击放大")
2. 选择Getter and Setter。

   ![](https://contentcenter-vali-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/pvt_2/DeveloperAlliance_scene_100_1/bc/v3/DF6vnQuCT2GXLuDnTOwu3A/zh-cn_image_0000002654795251.png "点击放大")
3. 选择要生成Getters和Setters的字段，点击OK，快速生成。

   ![](https://contentcenter-vali-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/pvt_2/DeveloperAlliance_scene_100_1/08/v3/PNS97rslQ56rxB6ZA_hWBw/zh-cn_image_0000002624635786.png "点击放大")
